Privacy

Your MIDI files stay on your device

Last updated 6 September 2026.

MIDI analysis

The checker reads MIDI files in your browser or desktop app. It does not upload, read, or retain your compositions.

License data

If you use Receipt mode, the app stores your license token and a dated verification result on your device.

Verification sends only that token to Sociobot’s billing API. Sociobot/Dodo handles checkout data under its own policy.

Website data

The site uses no advertising or analytics cookies. The hosting service may keep standard security logs, such as an IP address and request time.

Your control

Remove local licenses and preferences using your browser or operating system application-data controls.

Questions: privacy@sociobot.in.
